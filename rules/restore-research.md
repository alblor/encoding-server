# Soft pre-step and film grain: research digest (2026-10-30)

The owner asked for other restoration models as a SOFT step before ReCaVSR, plus re-adding cinematic noise. A research agent produced a sourced report; this is its digest, not a measurement of this project (labels: authors' claims unless marked). Grain measurements are in `restore-finish-grain-hdr.md`.

## Soft step: what is in, what is out

- hqdn3d, optionally with a deblock filter (block size 8), is the safest soft step: deterministic, cannot hallucinate. It is implemented as the recipe field `advanced.prefilter` (`none | light | medium | strong`, plus `deblock`) and the `cleanup-4k` preset uses `light`.
- DART (arXiv 2607.21219, MIT licence, 52 MB of weights): scratches, dust and flicker, aimed at monochrome film, trained on 256 px crops. Behaviour at 2160p is unverified. It is the first learned candidate to try on a clip (INQUIRY-045).
- BM3D with a temporal stage is a possible upgrade of the classical step (IDEA-046).
- RTN needs a Python 3.6 and torch 1.5 port. DeepRemaster is legacy. RVRT and BasicVSR++ need compiled operators rebuilt for sm_120. FastDVDnet only handles Gaussian noise. FBCNN is image-only JPEG deblocking.
- Ruled out: SeedVR2, All-In-One-Deflicker, Flickerformer, HaineiFRDM.
- Compression artifacts of low-quality encodes (INQUIRY-104, 2026-11-01): only the plateau purge (MIN-103) is safe; FBCNN, ffmpeg deband/deblock filters and ring suppression were rejected on measurements, numbers in `restore-finishing-features.md`. Blocking and ringing cannot be removed conservatively.

## Grain

Encoder-side estimation needs a grained input; baked noise works on every player but costs bitrate; TV support for AV1 film grain is unknown. What the tests showed is in `restore-finish-grain-hdr.md`.

## Bit depth

The research on learned or diffusion 8-to-10-bit models found NO open model for video; everything published is image-only. The recommendation stays float + classical deband + grain + one 16-bit rounding (`restore-finish-grain-hdr.md`), with `bitdepth.anchor_source` (MIN-051) as the unpublished idea for 10-bit sources.

Sony Research, "Generating HDR Video from SDR Video" (arXiv 2605.14703, repo SonyResearch/generative_sdr_to_hdr_video, Apache-2.0, created 2026-08-31), evaluated 2026-10-31 after the owner pointed to it. Correction: the first-session note "no released code" came from the paper page alone and was wrong; the code exists. Verified: 50-step diffusion on Wan2.2-TI2V-5B, 17-frame clips at 1280x704, 52.1 s and 36.5 GB peak per clip (GPU not stated), output EXR relative scene-linear radiance (no nits calibration, no PQ path), 9.4 GB checkpoints on a sync.com share that cannot be fetched headlessly, banding not addressed. Arithmetic, not measured: about 147 h per 2 h film at 1280x704 on the unknown reference GPU, at least 1,350 h at 2160p. Verdict (INQUIRY-062): not feasible at film scale and not a bit-depth method; at most an optional per-scene enhancer (low-resolution gain map), untested. The static BT.2408 mapping stays. Context: `polishing-log.md`.

## Faces: what was weighed for the uncanny small faces (2026-10-31, reports not kept)

Why I looked: the model draws wrong eyes on a face 40 to 60 px tall (`restore-face-guard.md`). The ReCaVSR zoom crop (arm A) was the first thing tested and it won, so everything else below is only worth testing if the owner wants REAL identity and not just a plausible face.

- Rejected for now (INQUIRY-087, complete): reference-based face restorers (ReF-LDM, InstantRestore, RestorerID and the like). They are non-commercial research code with old dependency pins, unproven at 40 to 60 px, and identity gating from a tiny face is the weak link. ComfyUI-style options: FaceDetailer works per frame, so it flickers; the DLSS5 enhancer is Windows-only with a closed runtime.
- KEEP, tested on the server (2026-10-31, torch 2.8 image, weights verified): worse than the ReCaVSR zoom in every variant, so rejected. The aligned 128 px input invents a different face; from source it gives waxy, doll-like faces and features drawn on the back of the head in profile frames; on top of the zoom it adds a frown and an expression change and 22 to 26% more flicker (Farneback-warped difference 1.98 against 1.62); on the fast zoom-in clip it pastes a sharp, sticker-like, male-looking face on the blurred head. 13.1 GB of reserved VRAM and an NTU S-Lab licence (non-commercial).
- Wan2.1-VACE (Apache-2.0, INQUIRY-088, complete 2026-11-01, dropped by the owner). The first test (control video = blurry crop, mask None) showed no effect of the references, since with mask=None the whole clip is a dense control. The RETEST with masked inpainting, white-background references and an SFace identity metric (session): 1.3B with ONE reference and a grey fill gives identity about 0.55 against 0.35 for the zoom, but it is fragile: three references were worse, a hard cut fails, the result is seed-unstable, and 14B in Q4 takes 6 to 7 min per clip. The owner dropped it. CodeFormer's gate test has no recorded result; open a new ID if he wants it.
- SparkVSR as the face engine (INQUIRY-101, complete 2026-11-01, measured on the server, session): NOT better than the ReCaVSR zoom. Without a reference (`no_ref`) the face is blurry with hollow eyes; it only matches the zoom when it is fed the zoom's own key-frames, through a local patch; flicker is not better. Cost: about 0.8 s per frame, 37 GiB RSS, a 256 px x 81-frame crop runs out of memory, and a track of 80 frames costs about 90 s extra. Quantisation was not run, since quality gave no benefit to buy speed for. The SparkVSR weights, code, image and scratch were deleted from the server on 2026-11-01 with the owner's approval (MIN-107).
- MiniMax-H3 (INQUIRY-099, planned, NOT researched; only if the owner asks): `MiniMaxAI/MiniMax-H3`, 33B dense DiT, omni-modal, 768p, Ref2VA takes up to 9 reference images; the open weights have no video-to-video, its context preprocessing and 2K are hosted-only, int8 needs 42.5 GB. Real and open-weight, but the community licence excludes the EU, UK, Korea and the US and forbids using outputs outside the territory. The owner (Italy) said quantised copies exist and asked for research (also on Reddit) only if VACE fails, and VACE was dropped instead; I told him the licence restriction once and that the technical fit looks weak.
- Other candidates: "Germini from ByteDance" most plausibly means Bernini (`bytedance/Bernini`, Apache-2.0, Wan-based, rv2v with reference images, not face-specific, a small model weak on humans); that reading is an inference, unverified. RG-FVR (weights not released), Stand-In (Tencent, Apache-2.0), ReF-LDM (image-level, non-commercial). For scale, reference-based restorers reach ArcFace similarity 0.676 with references against 0.231 without on synthetic x8-32 degradation (ReF-LDM paper, image level, not video).
- Sony SDR-to-HDR was already closed as not feasible (INQUIRY-062, above).
