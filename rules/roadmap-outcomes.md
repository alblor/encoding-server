- INQUIRY-038 (2026-10-30, closed as measured): FP8 on the real ReCaVSR transformer. Per-tensor FP8 on the middle 26 blocks is transparent (48.4 dB against bf16, two bf16 runs differ by 51 dB) and frees about 3 GiB (peak 10.4 against 13.2 GiB), but it is not faster: eager FP8 0.648 s/frame against compiled bf16 0.573, because `torch.compile` hangs on the quantized model. Rowwise FP8 fails on this consumer Blackwell (`CUBLAS_STATUS_NOT_SUPPORTED`). Outcome: bf16 default, `precision: fp8_fast` stays an experimental advanced option. NVFP4 and MXFP8 are not offered (owner: NVFP4 overkill; MXFP8 kernels not built for this part). Numbers: `restore-measurements.md`.
- INQUIRY-062 (2026-10-31, closed as not feasible): the Sony paper "Generating HDR Video from SDR Video" (arXiv 2605.14703) has code (Apache-2.0) after all, but it is a 50-step diffusion on Wan2.2-TI2V-5B at 1280x704 with 52.1 s per 17-frame clip, EXR scene-linear output without nits calibration, checkpoints not downloadable headlessly, and it does not address banding. By arithmetic about 147 h per 2 h film at 1280x704, at least 1,350 h at 2160p. The static BT.2408 mapping stays. Details: `restore-research.md`, `polishing-log.md` item 2.
- MIN-063 (2026-10-31): the wrong "no released code" claim came from reading the paper page and not the repository; the lesson (verify absence claims in the source of truth) is saved in the agent memory.
- INQUIRY-061 (2026-10-31, closed as adopted): FP8 re-investigation. Own `fp8` mode (`Fp8Linear`, per-channel weights x per-token activations, tensor-wise `_scaled_mm`, needs torch.compile): DiT 1.43x (18.21 to 12.77 s), 0.578 to 0.475 s/frame, VRAM 13.2 to 9.7 GiB, 46.83 dB against bf16 (worst frame 45.9), gate passed. Error grows along a segment and resets at each cut; hardware ceiling about 3x. Torchao's hang was its global Inductor config. No single FP8 matmul exceeds about 5.3 effective bits (bf16 9.2); three reach 9.1, break-even (the owner's 9-bit question). Built as MIN-070 (`restore-measurements.md`).
- INQUIRY-087 (2026-10-31 night, closed as rejected for now): reference-based face restorers (ReF-LDM, InstantRestore, RestorerID and the like) and the ComfyUI options (FaceDetailer flickers per frame, the DLSS5 enhancer is Windows-only with a closed runtime). The leftovers are INQUIRY-088. Details: `restore-research.md`, `restore-face-guard.md`.

- INQUIRY-101 (2026-11-01, closed as not better): SparkVSR as the small-face engine against the ReCaVSR zoom (session). `no_ref` is blurry with hollow eyes; it matches the zoom only when fed the zoom's own key-frames through a local patch; flicker is not better. - INQUIRY-104 (2026-11-01, closed): conservative removal of compression artifacts. Safe: only the plateau purge (MIN-103), measured on synthetic degradations of clean v8 frames (banding curvature 0.051 to 0.027, textured-area change 0.006 to 0.016 codes, high-frequency energy ratio 0.999 to 1.000). Rejected: `deband` (81 to 87% of a clean frame changed), ffmpeg gradfun, deband, spp, fspp, uspp, deblock, FBCNN (high-frequency ratio 0.81 to 0.95), ring suppression (no effect), gated deblocking (safe, modest, not shipped). Blocking and ringing are not solvable conservatively. Evidence is synthetic plus eyeballed sheets.
